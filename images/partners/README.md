The partner hierarchy follows the organisational team updates supplied by the site owner on 8 and 12 October 2026. Earlier contributors were restored under Prize & Raffle Supporters, then moved to their confirmed roles in the 12 October update. InterContinental Wellington is intentionally recognised twice: as an Event Experience Partner sponsoring One Mo' Night and as a Prize & Raffle Supporter.

The homepage distinguishes “In support of Movember” and “Powered by lululemon” from Momentum (Campaign Support Partner) and Gazley / YouDo / Elyte Hydration (Flagship Event Partners). Toitū Pōneke — The Hub is the Flagship Venue. Keep those relationships consistent on both pages.

The full role-based list lives in `partners.html`. Aye Empanadas and MRG Civil are Mo Unfiltered supporters; Urban Company is the same supporter as MRG Civil and must not be listed separately. Datacom has been removed. Fisiocrem is a Product / Service Partner, FMG is a Community Partner and Go Media is an Event Experience Partner.

O-Studio uses the contribution line supplied in the update. Flight Coffee’s support for Move for Movember is established in homepage event copy. Other descriptions stay at the confirmed role level; do not infer specific services, funding amounts or deliverables without confirmation. Product/service and prize/raffle supporters share group introductions. Keep contact details, payment status and internal notes out of public copy.
