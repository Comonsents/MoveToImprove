Wellington · Flagship Event

Saturday 21 November 2026 · 5am-midday

One morning.
Many ways
to move.

Run one loop or all seven, walk with friends, join a class or cheer on your community. There’s a place for you at our biggest event of the month.

01 / GETTING HERE

Start here.
We’ll guide you.

Toitū Pōneke — The Hub
49 Kilbirnie Crescent, Kilbirnie, Wellington

Enter through the main doors and head upstairs to registration in the South Lounge, our main event room. Accessible access is available, and volunteers will be there to guide you.

Registration and ticket check-in

Please have your Humanitix ticket and QR code ready on your phone for our volunteers to scan. Your ticket is in your Humanitix confirmation email. If you’ve booked more than one activity, have the relevant tickets available for each check-in.

Runners and walkers: registration opens in the South Lounge 30 minutes before your chosen hourly start. Be checked in and ready to leave with your lead 15 minutes before the hour. Your group lead will brief you and walk with you to the wind sculpture near Evans Bay, using the pedestrian crossings. Both runs and walks start at the wind sculpture on the hour. Please register at the venue first.

Movement classes: bring the Humanitix ticket for each booked class. Book a separate $5 ticket for each class; free General Admission does not reserve a class place. Class registration opens 15 minutes before the class starts and closes when it begins.

Kids Zone: caregivers must sign children in and out at the Kids Zone in the North Lounge.

Can’t find your ticket? Ask a registration volunteer for help.

Street parking is available in surrounding Kilbirnie streets. There is no dedicated event car park, so please allow extra time.

A bag drop is available at registration, with volunteers helping store belongings. Keep valuables with you. Changing rooms are also available at the venue.

02 / THE HOURLY LOOPS

One loop. A few.
All seven.

Our backyard-ultra-inspired challenge offers seven approximately 6.7km loops, starting hourly from 5am through to 11am. Join one, a few or all seven for up to approximately 46.9km. It’s about showing up and supporting each other.

Prefer to walk? Join a guided 30-minute walk on the same route: 15 minutes out and 15 minutes back, at a comfortable pace. Your walk lead will guide the turnaround; you don’t need to complete the full loop.

Runs and walks use the same hourly starts and check-in times below. Meet your lead in the South Lounge before walking together to the start.

If you need to stop, withdraw or return separately, tell your group lead so everyone is accounted for. Follow the briefing, use designated crossings and share public paths respectfully.

THE ROUTE + START POINT

Know the route.
Find your start.

Explore the approximately 6.7km route below, or open it on Strava for a closer look. Your group lead will explain the route and crossings at the briefing.

Check in at the South Lounge 15 minutes before your start. Your lead will walk with you to Zephyr, the wind sculpture near Evans Bay where the runs and walks begin. Look for the tall orange wind wand pictured below.

The route in words: from the wind sculpture, follow the Evans Bay waterfront north past Hataitai and Roseneath to the halfway marker shown on the map, then return along the same route. The full run is approximately 6.7km. Your lead will explain the crossings and turnaround at the briefing.

Walkers follow their lead for 15 minutes out and 15 minutes back; the halfway marker on the map is for the full running route.

05 / ON THE DAY

Come prepared.
Stay connected.

Make time to recover, refuel and connect between activities. Our volunteers and event team will be there to help you find your way.

  • Recovery + reset

    O-Studio’s recovery experiences are first come, first served on the day, subject to availability and the team’s guidance. StretchLab appointments are fully booked. If you have a booking, follow your confirmation instructions.

  • Food + hydration

    Food will be available to purchase, including our bake sale. Elyte Hydration will provide water stations at the venue and the wind sculpture. Bring your own water bottle.

  • What to bring

    Wear comfortable movement gear and familiar shoes. Bring a water bottle, weather layers, sun protection when needed, snacks or fuel, and any personal medication or equipment. Reflective or high-visibility clothing is sensible for early starts.

  • Help when you need it

    First aid will be based at Toitū Pōneke, proudly supported by Wellington Free Ambulance. Event staff will also be at the wind sculpture. Speak to a volunteer or team member if you need help. For an immediate emergency, call 111.

  • Weather + updates

    Check Move to Improve’s Instagram for last-minute updates. If the forecast is unfavourable, organisers will make a weather decision on the evening of Friday 20 November and share any changes there.

Photography and video may take place. Let the event team know if you would prefer not to be deliberately featured.
