01 / THE HOURLY LOOPS

One loop.
Or all Seven.

At the centre of the morning is a backyard ultra-inspired run and walk. A new loop starts every hour from 5:00am through to 11:00am.

Do one loop. Do a few. Take on all seven. Walk. Run. Stop when you’re ready.

02 / FIND YOUR WAY

More ways to
take part.

Alongside the loops, the event will include other ways to participate throughout the morning:

  • Run + walk

    One loop or all seven. The challenge is yours to choose.

  • Functional movement

    An accessible strength and functional workout for anyone who wants to move without taking on the run.

  • Pilates + yoga

    Lower-impact movement, mobility, breathwork and a chance to reset.

  • Recovery hub

    Stretch, recover and look after your body between activities or after you finish.

  • Community + partner village

    Coffee, community, useful resources, partner experiences and plenty of space to stay, support others and connect.

03 / YOU BELONG HERE

Show up your way.
Every movement counts.

You don’t have to complete seven loops for your contribution to matter.

Come for an hour or spend the morning with us. Move, volunteer, cheer, recover, fundraise or simply be part of the community.

Route and registration details will be announced soon.
