01 / ABOUT

A Wellington Movember
campaign

Movement brings us together. Connection helps us improve.

Move to Improve is not an elite fitness event. It is an open invitation to move, connect, contribute, and create space for honest conversations about men’s health.

No one should have to carry everything alone. Moving together makes it easier to talk—and every contribution helps create meaningful change.

03 / THE MONTH

A month of
movement
& connection.

Move to Improve is our Wellington Movember campaign, with events running from 31 October to 1 December 2026 and the main campaign activity taking place throughout November.

Four key events bring the community together through movement, conversation, fundraising and connection — beginning with Move for Movember on the eve of November and finishing with One Mo' Night once the month is complete.

You don’t need to be a runner or take on the biggest challenge to be part of it. Come for one event or join us throughout the campaign.

Show up your way. Every movement counts.

Event 01Start together

Move for Movember

Saturday 31 October · 8:00am
Gazley Motors

We’re starting the campaign together, one day before Movember officially begins.

More detailsHide details about Move for Movember

Join runners, walkers, local run clubs and the wider Wellington community as we kick off Move to Improve and get ready for a month of movement, fundraising and conversation.

This is an easy, social way to get involved, meet the people behind the campaign and start with movement, coffee and connection.

We’ll also share how you can take part throughout November, create your Movember fundraising page and support the campaign.

Event 02Make space

Mo Unfiltered

Wednesday 11 November · 6:00pm
ANZ Centre, 171 Featherston Street

Movement brings us together, but sometimes the most important part is what happens when we stop and talk.

Sign up →
More detailsHide details about Mo Unfiltered

Mo Unfiltered is an evening centred on men’s health, mental health, lived experience and the value of having honest conversations with the people around us.

This isn’t a formal seminar or a fitness event. It’s a relaxed community gathering designed to create space to listen, connect and talk about some of the things men often find difficult to discuss.

There’s no expectation to share. Come along, listen, meet others and be part of the conversation.

Strong communities create space for honest conversations.

Proudly supported by Aye Empanadas, Urban Company and No Ugly.

Event 04Close strong

One Mo' Night

Tuesday 1 December · 6:00pm
Two Grey

One final chance to come together after Movember.

Sign up →
More detailsHide details about One Mo' Night

Following a month of movement, conversations, fundraising and community, we’ll close the 2026 campaign with a relaxed evening at Two Grey.

It’s a chance to celebrate what we’ve achieved together, recognise the people and organisations who helped make Move to Improve possible, share our fundraising result and reflect on the month.

Expect campaign highlights, raffle prizes, good food and drink, and plenty of time to reconnect with the people you’ve met throughout the campaign.

Whether you ran a loop, joined an event, volunteered, donated, shared your story or simply showed up, this evening is for everyone who played a part.

Move together. Improve together.

Proudly sponsored by InterContinental Wellington.
