01 / ABOUT

A Wellington Movember
campaign

Movement brings us together. Connection helps us improve.

Move to Improve is not an elite fitness event. It is an open invitation to move, connect, contribute, and create space for honest conversations about men’s health.

No one should have to carry everything alone. Moving together makes it easier to talk—and every contribution helps create meaningful change.
